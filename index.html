Reminders are sent to the ntfy app on your phone. Install “ntfy” from the Play Store, then subscribe to the exact topic below.

Timing

You get a digest of what's due tomorrow and today, plus a separate alert for each overdue item.

Note presets

Tap a note field to see these as one-tap chips. Anything you type by hand is learned automatically and joins the list.

Highlight colours

Yellow means you can action it now, pink means you're waiting on someone. Rename or add more as you like.

This device

Text size and theme are kept on this device only, so your phone can be large and dark while a monitor stays normal and light.
